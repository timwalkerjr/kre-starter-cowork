<p class="font-sans text-base text-foreground leading-relaxed mb-6">
Administrators spoke last week about the influence the three have had since coming on board and the connection they are making with students.

<blockquote class="border-l-4 border-primary pl-6 mb-6">
<p class="font-sans text-base text-foreground leading-relaxed italic">

</p>
<cite class="block mt-3 font-display text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-widest text-primary">
— Paula Husar, High School Principal
</cite>
</blockquote>

<p class="font-display text-xs font-bold uppercase tracking-widest text-muted-foreground mb-4">[ Superintendent's Statement ]</p>
<p class="font-sans text-base text-foreground leading-relaxed mb-4">
"They will have routine safety and security duties," Palmerton's acting Superintendent Dr. Al Lonoconus said. "We want them to become a presence in the schools, be in the hallways working alongside our regular school personnel."
</p>
<p class="font-sans text-base text-foreground leading-relaxed mb-4">
At the junior high school, Principal Rich DeSocio said, things are off to an equally great start and students can be seen high-fiving the officer in the hallway and talking with him during lunch periods.
</p>
<p class="font-sans text-base text-foreground leading-relaxed">
"I know our discipline numbers were down slightly in December," Husar said, "so hopefully it will continue to have a positive impact all around."
